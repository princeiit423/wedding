Congratulations! Planning a wedding down to the last detail can feel overwhelming — the best way to ease that is to begin as early as possible.

For destination weddings, an ideal time frame is anywhere from 12 to 18 months in advance.

Grand-Scale Wedding (400+ guests)

Allow at least 18 months — this ensures guest availability, visa processing, hotel bookings, and international logistics.

Intimate Destination Wedding (up to 250 guests)

A 12-month timeline works comfortably for domestic destination weddings with a smaller guest count.

That said, our team has delivered breathtaking weddings on shorter timelines — reach out and we'll work with your schedule.

Budget & Costs

Transparent answers about how we charge, how to build a realistic wedding budget, and how BlueBuds helps you get the best value for your celebration.

At BlueBuds, we transparently charge a management fee for the wedding. The exact fee depends on the scale of the wedding and the overall scope of services required.

We will walk you through all costs clearly during your consultation — no hidden charges, no surprises.

Our team creates a detailed, itemised budget tailored to your celebration. To arrive at the most accurate plan, we ask for a few details upfront:

  • A list of all wedding functions planned
  • A tentative guest list or rough guest count
  • Any must-have wishlist items for your wedding
  • One or two pre-agreed wedding date options (which affect hotel and travel pricing by season)

Once we have these, our planning experts create a comprehensive budget with a detailed breakdown of every element — and guide you to make the best decisions to stay on track and get the best value.

Absolutely. By choosing the right wedding planner, you can efficiently allocate your budget towards your true priorities and avoid costly mistakes.

BlueBuds' extensive network of suppliers and luxury hotel partnerships allows us to secure competitive rates that may be inaccessible to you otherwise. Our industry connections also mean that in case of any unexpected situation, we can swiftly make last-minute arrangements without compromising quality.

01 — Establish a Realistic Budget

Setting a practical budget first allows your planner to develop a feasible plan, including destination and venue options.

02 — Efficient Supplier Management

Our team ensures suppliers are engaged at favourable rates — maximising quality while preventing uncontrolled cost escalation.

03 — Thoughtful Guest List Management

An intimate, focused guest list means more attention to quality in food, décor, and personalisation.

04 — Select an Off-Peak Wedding Date

Opting for an off-season or shoulder-season date can unlock the best rates and packages at premium venues.
